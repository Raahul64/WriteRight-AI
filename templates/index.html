AI Grammar & Text Improver

Pretrained NLP models: T5 grammar correction, SymSpell, TextStat

About this project

Problem: Many people make grammar, spelling and clarity mistakes, and good tools are paid or need an internet API.

Objective: A free, open-model writing assistant that corrects, explains and rewrites text.

AI technique: Transformer seq2seq models (T5) for grammar correction and style rewriting; SymSpell edit-distance spell checking; NLTK sentence tokenization; TextStat readability.

Limitations: Small models can miss errors or change meaning; first request is slow while models load; error types are heuristic labels; English only.
